What Wisp can do in Chrome

Wisp only does what you ask, and everything stays on this Mac. The extension talks to Wisp over 127.0.0.1 and nowhere else.

Tabs Always on

See, group, pin, mute, focus and close your tabs. Tab titles and addresses only, never what's on the page.

You can change these any time here, or in Chrome's own extension settings. Incognito windows are never visible to Wisp.
