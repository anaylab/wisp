# Wisp: Congressional App Challenge demo video script

Recorded video, about 3:00. Everything below the main script is optional material to
swap in or use for Q&A. Claims marked (verify) have not been tested live yet.

## The one-line pitch
Wisp is an AI companion that lives in the Mac's notch. It sees your screen, teaches you
on the page, and does things for you. It runs on your own Mac by default, and anything
that sends, deletes or pays always asks first.

## Key points to land (in this order)
1. It sees your screen and you just talk to it (push-to-talk, ctrl+option).
2. It teaches on the page: handwritten working, circles, step-through, Save Worked Solution.
3. It guides you through unfamiliar apps step by step, and can do a step for you.
4. It acts for you: windows, tabs, texts, email drafts, browser tasks.
5. It always asks first for anything irreversible, and you can edit the text before approving.
6. It is alive: Buddies (helpers with jobs, memory, limits) and Automations.
7. Private by default: local model, on-device speech, no analytics. Cloud is opt-in and labelled.

## Recording setup
- Record the screen silently (Cmd+Shift+5, 1080p+), one clip per beat, retake freely.
- Record voiceover afterwards. The mic is used by push-to-talk, so do not narrate live.
- Keep Wisp's own spoken replies and sound cues; duck them under narration.
- Trim the roughly 4s Claude start-up per reply, or cover it with a zoom or caption.
- Caption each hotkey and spoken command, since viewers cannot hear you hold ctrl+option.
- Do Not Disturb on, other apps quit, clean desktop and menu bar.
- Pre-stage: a YouTube lesson paused on a hard problem, about 15 messy Chrome tabs
  (with duplicates), a "mom" contact (or your own number), Wisp Tabs extension loaded.
- Model: Claude (your subscription) for everything; switch to local only for the privacy clip, last.

## Alternate opener (10 seconds)
Say "play some music" (Apple Music, not Spotify) as a warm-up, noting it never touches a
model. Weaker than tabs as a hook; use only if you have spare seconds.

## Optional segments (swap in, or keep for Q&A)
- **Email drafts:** "Draft an email to Sam about moving Friday's meeting." An editable
  composer opens; nothing sends until Cmd+Return.
- **Browser control:** "Go to Wikipedia and search for ..." It works in its own tab and
  asks before anything irreversible. Keep to one safe task; never demo a purchase.
- **Screen watchers:** "Tell me when the build finishes." (verify)
- **Timers and glance cards:** "Set a timer for 5 minutes"; weather, places, calendar cards.
- **Ask About a Part:** drag-select a region, or say "explain the third answer."
- **Automations:** trigger, source, action, output, notify. Say "every weekday morning ..."
  and an editable draft card appears. Eight templates in the gallery.
- **Canvas and daily checklist:** paste a Canvas calendar feed; it becomes a tickable
  checklist. Study blocks are written only after you approve one card. Needs the feed URL.
- **Morning notch:** a daily banner built with no model call.
- **Brain:** a local wiki of your notes with one-click undo.
- **Learned shortcuts, Chrome tab muting, pinning, and so on:** mention only if asked.

## Safety and privacy talking points
- Local-first: on-device speech (Parakeet), on-device voice (Kokoro), local model option,
  no analytics.
- Cloud models are bring-your-own-key or your Claude subscription, opt-in, labelled as
  leaving the Mac.
- Destructive-action floor: send, delete, pay, share, publish and arbitrary commands
  always ask, even with auto-approve on.
- Passwords and payment fields are refused by the browser agent.
- Canvas feed URL is stored only in the Keychain.

## Tips for judges
- State the problem in the first 10 seconds.
- Make every clip work with the sound off (captions).
- Cut anything that does not record cleanly instead of apologizing.
- End card: your name, district, project name.
- Add a real user story if you have one: "I built this because ___ struggled with ___."

## Before recording: things to verify on screen
- Tab grouping, Save Worked Solution, and the guided walkthrough on YouTube.
- Routine and shortcut keys, if you add a "pick a number" bit.
- A Claude-subscription reply end to end (about 4s start-up per reply).
- The local model is weaker than Claude; keep that clip simple.
