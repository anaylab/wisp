# Wisp demo video script (target ~2:45, check the official time limit)

Setup: Settings → Model → "Claude (your subscription)". Say on camera that this is the model for the demo.
Rule: say everything in your own words. Replace [brackets]. Record live, no cuts over errors.

| Time | On screen | You say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 **Problem** | A worksheet or hard problem on screen | "[One statistic, with source.] When you're stuck, help is rarely where you're working. Wisp is a helper built into my Mac that sees my screen and explains." |
| 0:20–0:40 **What it is** | Notch appears, hold Control+Option | "Wisp lives in the notch. I hold two keys, ask out loud, and let go. By default everything runs privately on my Mac. For this demo I switched on a smarter option: my own Claude account, which sends my screen and question to Anthropic. It's off unless I choose it." |
| 0:40–1:25 **Demo 1: math** | Drag-select the system of equations, ask "walk me through it" | Notes appear step by step, click Next. "It works the problem beside the problem and circles the answer, C: x = 2, y = −2. Then I can save it as a study sheet." Click Save Worked Solution. |
| 1:25–1:50 **Demo 2: windows** | Say "put Safari on the left and Notes on the right" | "No AI needed for this. It rearranges my windows so the worksheet and my notes sit side by side, instantly." |
| 1:50–2:05 **Demo 3: safety** | A confirmation card (email Send or a text) | "It never sends, deletes or pays without asking me first." |
| 2:05–2:30 **Code** | 10–15 lines you chose | "This is how [window placement / the safety check] works: [plain words]. I wrote [X]; [library] does [Y]." |
| 2:30–2:45 **Impact** | Tester + quote | "[N] students tried it. [Quote.] Next, [goal]. Thank you." |

## Do before recording
- Run the math problem 5 times with the subscription model. Pick the cleanest run's behavior and keep the same wording.
- Expect about 4 seconds of delay per reply. Don't cut it; say "it's thinking" or talk over it.
- Skip the airplane-mode proof (it needs the local model). If you want a privacy shot, show the Model settings screen instead.
- Close notifications; hide messages, email and contacts.
