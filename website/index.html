Questions.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac on macOS 26 or later. Apple silicon with 16 GB of memory is recommended for the local model.

Does it watch my screen all the time?

No. Wisp captures your screen only when you press the shortcut or type a question.

Is it free?

Joining is free, and the local setup needs no subscription. Cloud models use your own API key.

When do I get in?

We're inviting people in small batches. Join the list and we'll email you when your spot opens.
