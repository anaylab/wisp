02 · The team

A small studio, hard at work
while you're away.

Each buddy owns one job, with its own chat, memory, model and tools. They run on a schedule, hand their results to each other, and only interrupt you when something needs your OK.

The Briefer

Reads the calendar and mail each morning and writes three lines on what matters. Then it hands the brief on.

Hand off the answer

When one buddy finishes its job, the buddies you picked get the answer and start on their own. A buddy with several inputs waits for all of them.

Ask the team

Ask up to four teammates at once, split work into side threads, or draft a new teammate with your OK.

Threads side by side

Every chat runs on its own. A message sent mid-job can steer the work in progress.

Backup models

If a model hits a limit or goes down, the next one takes over.

A session of its own

Run a buddy on your Claude subscription with a kept session per conversation.

Tidies its own notes

Every 20 messages a buddy reviews what it remembered and fixes it. Notes you wrote are never touched.

04 · Privacy

Nothing leaves.
By design.

Your Mac is the whole pipeline. By default, nothing about your screen, voice or words leaves this machine.

Nothing leaves.

Your voice, your screen and the model's answer never leave this Mac. There is no route out, so there is nothing to switch off.

Local first

The default model runs on your Mac through Ollama. Brain pages are plain markdown files on your disk.

Cloud is opt-in

Claude, GPT and Gemini are bring-your-own-key, or use your Claude subscription. Wisp labels clearly when a screenshot would leave your Mac.

Nothing to track you

No account, no analytics code, no telemetry. Keys and feed URLs live in your login Keychain. The usage log keeps counts, never content.

By default, stays on your Mac

  • Your voice and speech-to-text
  • Your screen, read by Accessibility and OCR
  • The model's replies, through Ollama
  • Your brain pages and buddy memory
  • Your keys, in your login Keychain

Only if you opt in

  • Prompts to Claude, GPT or Gemini with your own key
  • A screenshot, and Wisp labels it clearly first
  • Apps you connect through MCP or Composio
  • Nothing else. No account, no analytics, no telemetry

06 · Before you try it

Good questions,
plainly answered.

Privacy & control

What leaves my Mac?

By default, nothing. Speech to text, voice, OCR and the local model all run on-device. The only network use by default is a one-time download of the speech models and optional weather and places lookups, which send a place name or query, never a screenshot. If you pick a cloud model, your transcript and screenshots go to that provider, and Wisp says so.

Can it do something I didn't want?

Anything that sends, deletes, pays, posts, invites or runs a command shows a card first, even if you turned on auto-approve, and you can edit the wording before you allow it. Password and payment fields in the browser are refused outright. Scheduled automations only get tools that never prompt.

Using Wisp

Is it really free?

Yes. Wisp is open source under the AGPL-3.0 license, and the default setup costs nothing to run because the model, speech and voice are all on your Mac. If you choose a cloud model, you pay that provider with your own key or subscription.

Which Macs does it need?

An Apple-silicon Mac on macOS 26 or later, since the notch uses Liquid Glass. 16 GB of memory is recommended for the local model.

Does it work in my apps?

Pointing, walkthroughs and screen reading work anywhere: native apps through Accessibility and everything else through on-device OCR. Browser control works with Chrome and Safari.

Why isn't this notarized?

Notarizing needs Apple's paid developer program. The install command downloads with curl, which macOS doesn't flag, and checks a checksum and signature first. The .dmg works too, after one right-click approval the first time. A notarized build may come later.
